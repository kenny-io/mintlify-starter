Google organizes all your work inside projects — self-contained spaces where tasks, members, deadlines, and progress live together. Before you invite your team or build automations, you need at least one project to anchor everything. This guide walks you through every step, from clicking **New Project** to watching real-time progress in the dashboard.

<Tip>
  Speed up setup by starting from a project template. On the **New Project** screen, select **Browse Templates** to find pre-built structures for software sprints, marketing campaigns, hiring pipelines, and more. Templates pre-populate tasks, statuses, and custom fields so you can skip the blank-canvas phase entirely.
</Tip>

## Create your first project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Projects hub">
    Log in to Google and click **Projects** in the left-hand navigation sidebar. If this is your first project, you'll land on an empty state screen. If your workspace already has projects, they appear as cards in a grid or list view.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the new project dialog">
    Click the **+ New Project** button in the top-right corner of the Projects hub. A creation dialog slides open with three configuration tabs: **Details**, **Visibility**, and **Members**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the project details">
    Complete the **Details** tab before moving on:

    - **Project name** — Enter a clear, descriptive name (e.g., *Q3 Product Launch* or *Engineering Sprint 12*). Names appear in navigation, notifications, and reports, so keep them specific.
    - **Description** — Write one or two sentences explaining the project's purpose. This text shows on the project card and helps teammates understand scope at a glance.
    - **Color & icon** — Pick a color and emoji icon to make the project visually distinct in the sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Switch to the **Visibility** tab and choose who can see the project:

    | Option | Who can access |
    |---|---|
    | **Private** | Only explicitly invited members |
    | **Team** | Everyone in your Google workspace |
    | **Public** | Anyone with the project link |

    Most teams start with **Team** visibility and restrict individual tasks as needed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first tasks">
    Click **Create Project** to save the details, then land on the project's task list. Select **+ Add Task** and enter a task name. Press **Enter** to save it and immediately create the next one. Add as many tasks as you need — you can always reorganize them later.

    For each task you can optionally set:
    - A **description** with rich text, links, and file attachments
    - A **priority** level (Urgent, High, Normal, Low)
    - A **status** (To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign tasks to team members">
    Open any task by clicking its name, then click the **Assignee** field in the right-hand details panel. Search for a teammate by name or email and select them. You can assign multiple people to a single task — the first person selected becomes the primary owner shown in list views.

    <Note>
      Team members must already be invited to your Google workspace before you can assign them to tasks. Go to **Settings → Members** to send workspace invitations.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set due dates">
    Still inside the task detail panel, click the **Due Date** field and pick a date from the calendar picker. Google automatically highlights overdue tasks in red and surfaces them in the **My Work** dashboard so assignees never miss a deadline.

    To set due dates in bulk, return to the task list, hold **Shift** and click to select multiple tasks, then right-click and choose **Set Due Date**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor progress in the dashboard">
    Click **Overview** in the project's top navigation bar to open the progress dashboard. You'll see:

    - A **completion ring** showing the percentage of tasks marked Done
    - A **status breakdown** bar chart across all task statuses
    - An **activity feed** of recent changes, comments, and file uploads
    - A **timeline summary** flagging tasks due in the next seven days

    Return to this view at any time to get a live snapshot of where your project stands.
  </Step>
</Steps>
