Google is a modern platform built for teams that want to move faster and work smarter. Whether you're coordinating a product launch, tracking engineering sprints, or managing cross-functional initiatives, Google gives you a single place to create projects, assign tasks, collaborate with your team, and measure progress in real time. Powerful automation rules eliminate repetitive manual work, while deep integrations with the tools you already use — Slack, GitHub, Zapier, and more — keep your entire workflow connected and in sync.

## How it works

Start by creating a workspace for your organization, then add projects to represent the work your teams own. Inside each project, break work down into tasks, assign owners, and set due dates to keep everyone accountable. From there, layer on automation rules to handle status updates, notifications, and handoffs automatically — and connect your favorite tools so Google stays in sync with the rest of your stack.

## Pricing plans

Google offers three plans designed to grow with your team. The **Free** plan gives individuals and small teams core project management and collaboration features at no cost. The **Pro** plan unlocks advanced automations, priority integrations, and detailed analytics for teams that need more power. The **Enterprise** plan provides custom security controls, dedicated support, and unlimited workspaces for large organizations with complex needs.
