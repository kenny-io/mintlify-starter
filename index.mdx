## Get started in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.google.com](https://app.google.com) and verify your email address. You'll land in your personal dashboard right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace">
    Name your workspace, invite teammates, and assign roles. Use [Account Setup](/get-started/account-setup) for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first project">
    Head to the **Projects** tab and click **New Project**. Follow the [First Project guide](/guides/first-project) to see all the options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the API">
    Generate an API key in **Settings → API Keys** and make your first authenticated request. See the [Authentication guide](/get-started/authentication) for details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
